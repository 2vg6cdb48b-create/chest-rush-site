Chest Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Chest Rush is a free game made by Nathan Gent-Newbould. This page explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Chest Rush does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. Chest Rush has no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send any information about you or your device to anyone, including us.

Your game save

Your progress (troops, chests, gold, gems and settings) is saved only on your own iPhone. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes your save.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows reminders on your phone when a chest is ready to open. These reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases and money

There are no in-app purchases and no real money in Chest Rush. All gold and gems are earned by playing.

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the Chest Rush support page.
