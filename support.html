Chest Rush Support

Chest Rush is a free chest-opening card game: open chests, collect 60 troops, level them up and climb through ten arenas. No ads, no purchases and no real money.

Get help

Found a bug, or have an idea? Email gen2penguwin@gmail.com and say which iPhone you have. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your save, so please don't delete it if you want to keep your troops.

How do I get more gems? By playing: chests, the daily reward, quests, the Star Track, achievements and the Lucky Wheel all give gems. Nothing in the game is bought with real money.

What are the chances in each chest? In the game, go to Shop, then tap "Chances".

How do I turn off chest reminders? In the game, open Settings (the gear icon) and turn chest alerts off.

Privacy

Chest Rush collects no personal information. Read the privacy policy.
