Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

NG Gas Records is an app made by Nathan Gent-Newbould of NG Plumbing & Heating for Gas Safe registered engineers to fill in landlord gas safety records. This page explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: the app does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send any information to anyone, including us.

The records you create

Records contain details you enter, such as property addresses, landlord and tenant names, contact details and signatures. They are saved only on your own device. They leave it only when you choose to share a record (for example by emailing the PDF to a landlord) or save a backup file, and then they go only where you send them.

As the person entering other people's details, you are responsible for handling them properly, in line with UK data protection law.

Reminders

Due-date alerts are scheduled on your phone itself; no server is involved. Reminder emails to landlords are sent from your own email app and account, only when you press Send.

Deleting your data

You can delete records in the app at any time. Deleting the app deletes everything it has saved, so make a backup first if you need to keep your records.

Children

The app is for gas engineers and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
