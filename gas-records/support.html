Gas Safety Records Support

Gas Safety Records lets Gas Safe registered engineers fill in landlord gas safety records (CP12s) on their phone, sign them on screen and send them as a PDF.

Get help

Found a bug, or have an idea? Email gen2penguwin@gmail.com and say which iPhone you have. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Where are my records saved? On your phone only. Landlords must keep records for at least two years, so use Settings → Back up regularly and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files.

How do I move my records to a new phone? On the old phone, go to Settings → Back up and save the file to iCloud Drive (or email it to yourself). On the new phone, install the app, go to Settings → Restore from backup and pick that file.

How is the next due date worked out? 12 months from the date of the check. If you enter the previous record's due date and the check is done up to two months before it, the original anniversary date is kept, as Regulation 36A allows.

Why can't I issue a record? The app lists anything still missing above the Issue button: for example the property address, the landlord's details, each appliance's checks, or your signature and Gas Safe number.

Does the app make the checks for me? No. It's a way to write down and send your findings. You remain responsible for the checks you carry out and for what each record says.
