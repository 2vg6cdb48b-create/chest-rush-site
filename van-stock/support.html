Van Stock Support

Van Stock keeps track of the parts and materials in your van. Tap − when you use something and + when you restock, see what's running low, and share a restock list with your merchant or office.

Get help

Found a bug, or have an idea? Email gen2penguwin@gmail.com and say which iPhone you have. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my stock saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes your stock list, so use More → Back up to save a backup file to iCloud Drive or Files first.

How do I move my stock to a new phone? On the old phone, go to More → Back up and save the file to iCloud Drive (or email it to yourself). On the new phone, install Van Stock, go to More → Restore from backup and pick that file.

How does the Restock list work? Each item has a "Restock when down to" number. When the amount in the van drops to that number or below, the item appears on the Restock tab. If you also set a "Full amount", the app tells you how many to get.

Can I get my stock into Excel? Yes. Go to More → Export stock list. It makes a spreadsheet (CSV) file you can open in Excel or Numbers, or email to the office.

The barcode scanner doesn't open. The app needs camera access to scan. Go to your iPhone's Settings, find Van Stock and turn Camera on.
