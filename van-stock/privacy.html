Van Stock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Van Stock is an app made by Nathan Gent-Newbould for keeping track of the stock in your van. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Van Stock does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. Van Stock has no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send any information about you, your stock or your device to anyone, including us.

Your stock list

The items, quantities and history you enter are saved only on your own iPhone. They never leave your device unless you choose to share them, for example by exporting a spreadsheet, saving a backup file or sharing a restock list. When you do, they go only where you send them. Deleting the app deletes your stock list, so make a backup first if you want to keep it.

Camera

If you allow it, the app uses your camera only to read barcodes on parts. The camera picture is processed on your phone and is never saved or sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in your iPhone's Settings; the rest of the app still works without it.

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
